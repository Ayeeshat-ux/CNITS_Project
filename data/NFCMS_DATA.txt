SECTION 1: IYCF AND MDD INDICATORS
==================================================

WHO/UNICEF Minimum Dietary Diversity (MDD) for children 6-23 months:
A child meets MDD if they eat from at least 5 of these 8 food groups per day:
1. Breast milk
2. Grains, roots, tubers and plantains
3. Pulses (beans, peas, lentils), nuts and seeds
4. Dairy products (milk, infant formula, yogurt, cheese)
5. Flesh foods (meat, fish, poultry, organ meats)
6. Eggs
7. Vitamin A-rich fruits and vegetables
8. Other fruits and vegetables

Minimum Meal Frequency (MMF):
- Breastfed infants 6-8 months: at least 2 meals per day
- Breastfed children 9-23 months: at least 3 meals per day
- Non-breastfed children 6-23 months: at least 4 meals per day (including 2 milk feeds)

Minimum Dietary Diversity for Women (MDD-W):
A woman achieves MDD-W if she eats from at least 5 of 10 food groups:
1. Grains, white roots, tubers and plantains
2. Pulses (beans, peas, lentils)
3. Nuts and seeds
4. Milk and milk products
5. Meat, poultry and fish
6. Eggs
7. Dark green leafy vegetables
8. Other Vitamin A-rich fruits and vegetables
9. Other vegetables
10. Other fruits

SECTION 14: LACTATING WOMEN — INTAKE SUMMARY
==================================================

Keywords: breastfeeding, nursing mother, lactating women, milk supply, breast milk, nursing diet

From NFCMS 2021 national data:
- Mean energy intake for lactating women: 2,061 kcal/day (below recommended 2,500-2,800 kcal)
- Mean protein intake for lactating women: 53 g/day (below recommended 71 g)
- 66% of lactating women have inadequate protein intake (63% rural, 51% urban)
- Mean animal-source protein: 14 g/day for non-pregnant women
- Mean plant-source protein: 40 g/day for lactating women

Key insight: Most Nigerian nursing mothers are not eating enough energy or protein. Priority is to increase food quantity and diversity, especially adding animal-source foods.

Top food sources for lactating mothers (nationally):
- Energy: Palm olein, Rice, Palm oil, Maize products, Garri
- Protein: Rice, Maize products, Cowpea, Beef, Bread
- Iron: Condiments, Millet products, Rice, Pepper, Garri
- Calcium: Bread, Shellfish/crayfish, Fura da nono, Baobab powder, Soybean products
- Vitamin A: Palm oil, Banga, Palm olein, Mango
- Folate: Cowpea products, Cowpea/beans, Garri, Rice
- Vitamin B12: Mackerel, Sardine, Catfish, Beef
- Vitamin C: Pepper, Tomato, Mango, Onion
